About AttendMate

AttendMate is a simple and student-friendly attendance calculator designed to make attendance management easier.

Students often need to calculate their attendance before deciding how many lectures they can miss or how many lectures they need to attend to reach their required attendance percentage. Manually calculating these values can be confusing and time-consuming.

AttendMate solves this problem with simple and accurate calculations. Students can enter their total lectures, present lectures, and absent lectures to calculate their current attendance. They can also enter the number of future lectures they plan to attend and see their expected future attendance.

The website also allows students to choose their own target attendance percentage, with 75% as the default target, and calculates how many lectures they need to attend continuously to achieve that target.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to help students understand, calculate, and plan their lecture attendance quickly and easily.
